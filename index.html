WHO WE ARE

ABOUT THE FACULTY & DEPARTMENT

General information about the Faculty of Education and the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science Education.

FACULTY OF EDUCATION

The Faculty of Education supports the preparation of professional teachers, educational researchers and leaders through quality teaching, practical experience and academic development.

DEPARTMENT OF MATHEMATICS AND COMPUTER SCIENCE EDUCATION

The department combines mathematics education, computer science education, pedagogy and digital skills to prepare students for teaching, research and technology-supported learning.

VISION & MISSION

VISION: To develop competent, ethical and innovative educators with strong mathematical and computing knowledge.

MISSION: To provide quality education, practical training, research opportunities and technology-rich learning for future educators.

STUDENT GUIDANCE

EDUCATION STUDENTS

EDUCATION STUDENTS

Education students are future teachers, mentors, and leaders. Your work is about more than delivering lessons—it is about understanding learners, building inclusive classrooms, assessing growth, and helping students believe in themselves. Stay reflective, ethical, and adaptable. The impact you make may not always be immediate, but it lasts.

COMPUTER SCIENCE EDUCATION STUDENTS

You stand at the intersection of education and technology. You must learn how people learn while also mastering programming, algorithms, data, and digital systems. Your role is to make computing accessible, not intimidating—teaching problem-solving, computational thinking, and responsible technology use. Keep both the learner and the machine in mind: strong pedagogy plus strong CS content creates powerful learning.

MATHEMATICS EDUCATION STUDENTS

Mathematics Education students develop strong mathematical understanding together with the knowledge and skills needed to teach mathematics effectively. The Personal Portal provides a separate MTHED academic structure so that Mathematics Education students can access information and courses relevant to their programme.
